# Leaping Bunny Mobile Experience

Leaping Bunny is a mobile-first beauty companion that helps users discover, manage, and replenish cruelty-free beauty products. By combining verified products, smart replenishment reminders, automatic price comparison, exclusive brand partnerships, and a gamified loyalty experience where users grow a garden or care for a bunny, the platform transforms conscious beauty shopping into an engaging long-term habit.

## Technology
– Mobile-first responsive web application rather than a native app. 
– React or Next.js
– The experience should be optimized for speed, accessibility, and one-handed use.
– Responsive web experience optimized for desktop and mobile
–Hosting via Vercel (not yet deployed/configured; currently runs via `npm run dev` locally)

## Data
– The experience uses mock data for product and barcode information, certification status, ingredient details, retailer pricing, promotions, loyalty rewards, purchase history, and user-defined usage patterns.
– Household profiles allow products, reminders, and shopping needs to be assigned to different family members. Pricing information should show when it was last updated and distinguish between the lowest price and best overall value.

## Layout
– The home screen provides a quick, glanceable view of products running low, upcoming reminders, personalized offers, rewards progress, and household needs. A persistent bottom navigation includes Home, Shop, Scan, Rewards, and Profile, with scanning positioned as a primary action. Product pages clearly communicate certification status, ingredients, pricing options, usage estimates, and replenishment settings.
– Gamify loyalty by allowing the user to plant seeds or grow plants to signify ongong purchases and commiting to conscious, cruelty free buying

## Design
– Leaping Bunny should balance premium beauty aesthetics with subtle gamification.
– Organic illustrations, soft gradients, nature-inspired visuals, and gentle animations create a warm and engaging experience without feeling childish. The garden or bunny companion acts as a secondary engagement layer that motivates users to maintain conscious purchasing habits and return to the app regularly.

## Interactions
– Scan products to verify eligibility and compare pricing.
– Receive replenishment reminders based on usage patterns.
– Track purchases for yourself and household members.
– Complete challenges and earn rewards from partner brands.
– Grow a virtual garden or care for a bunny companion to visualize loyalty progress.
– Unlock exclusive offers, early-access products, and limited-time rewards through ongoing engagement.

## Nice to Haves
– AI-powered concierge available to help the user with anything as needed
